# Kisan Mitra v1.1.2 — A little guide for your farm

Native guided tours now cover each onboarding step, home, chat, calendar, weather, Crop Doctor, markets, schemes, notebook sections, exchange, profile, farm station and Saathi. Each guide highlights the actual Flutter control and offers Next, Back, Skip and Done. The compass in each screen’s header replays its guide.

Progress is versioned and stored on the phone, scoped to the Firebase account (session fallback for local mode). Interrupted guides resume; skipped and completed guides stay dismissed until replayed. These preferences do not sync between phone and website. Setup waits for the restored onboarding step before starting its guide.

Tours scroll mounted controls into view and track their layout. Unavailable controls have a bounded wait and are skipped. Background controls are blocked while a guide is open; Android back dismisses the guide, and backgrounding the app removes its overlay while retaining progress. Open modal routes and voice calls are not interrupted. Tour controls use haptic selection feedback and adapt to dark theme, safe areas and enlarged text. A guide never taps a highlighted control, submits farm data, requests location or starts recording.
