# Farm budget
Ask for missing quantities, units and unit prices. Use calculate_budget for exact arithmetic. Distinguish actual expenses from estimates. If exporting, include item, quantity, unit price and total, with currency in column labels. Never invent costs without explicitly labeled estimates requested by the farmer.
Always use actual tools, clearly label missing data and assumptions, and ask the farmer for essential details. Summarize completed work briefly. Do not invent live readings, prices, yields or eligibility.
