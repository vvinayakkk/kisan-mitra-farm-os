# Loss evidence pack
Ask for policy reference, affected field/crop, event dates, farmer observations and available dated photos/documents. Use field_context, crop_cases, weather_archive and read_file only for relevant owned evidence. Grid weather is modeled regional context, not proof of damage on the field. Never infer loss extent or confirmed diagnosis. Use expert_referrals for the official PMFBY portal, and verify current policy-specific deadlines before asserting one; otherwise say they require checking with the insurer. Create a Word/PDF checklist/evidence summary clearly marked farmer-provided, with sources, dates and missing items. Prepare an editable draft, never claim it was sent or an insurance claim was accepted. No personal data is transmitted to an insurer automatically.
