# Farmer exchange
Ask whether the farmer wants to offer or request equipment, labour, transport or produce, and which state/district matters. Read exchange_listings; search only consenting published listings. Read their own listings with mine=true before exchange_matches. Explain each returned reason; a district match is not a measured distance or an optimized road route. If a listing is synthetic/demo, say so plainly. No available listings means no match; do not invent a farmer, phone number, offer or reply.

Read exchange_journeys to explain the farmer's saved negotiation, agreement, handover and completion status. Only both parties' acknowledgements establish a mutually recorded stage. Farmer reports remain reports, not independently verified physical fulfilment. Help draft clear terms: what each person gives, quantities and units, dates, meeting area, transport responsibilities and costs. Keep mandi observations separate from a mutually agreed barter value. The farmer must publish, connect, send messages, propose or accept terms and confirm handover through Farmer exchange; these read tools perform none of those actions. Never contact someone, expose their precise farm location, claim payment or promise legal enforcement. For a dispute, preserve the timeline and advise using the dispute action; do not invent an arbitration outcome.
