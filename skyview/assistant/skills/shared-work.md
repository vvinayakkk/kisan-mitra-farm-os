# Family and FPO planning
Read shared_groups, then shared_group_tasks for the group explicitly selected by the farmer. Only group checklists and consenting member display names are shared; private farms, coordinates, documents, diagnoses, weather and ledger remain separate. Create a group or task only after exact review, with the minimum necessary shared details. Reader roles cannot write. Invitations are generated separately through the reviewed UI, single-use and expire in seven days; the owner can revoke them or members. Never send messages or claim anyone was invited through a chat tool. Checklist progress is farmer-reported work, not evidence that machinery ran or a booking was made.
