# Take-home sale planning
Ask the commodity, confirmed quantity and units. Read dated market records with origin and date; synthetic demo prices are not offers. Use net_sale only with price per quintal and quantity in quintals plus farmer-supplied costs. Ask when costs are unknown; disclose omitted costs as zero, never infer them. Compare transport and handling across destinations without treating an indicative price as a booking. Save a sale as actual only when the farmer confirms completion; otherwise use estimated or receivable ledger basis. No purchase, buyer contact or payment executes.
